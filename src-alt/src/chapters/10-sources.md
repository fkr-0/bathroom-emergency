# Sources

Emergency routes, German service information, and medical, legal-aid,
preparedness, and postpartum material were reviewed for **22 July 2026**.
Official and primary sources are preferred. A link is not evidence by itself;
the guide records what each source supports and what it does not support.

## Emergency services and protocols

1. **gesund.bund.de.** "Nummern fuer den Notfall." Official German distinctions
   among 112, 110, 116 117, poison centres, and crisis routes.
   https://gesund.bund.de/notfallnummern

2. **TelefonSeelsorge.** Around-the-clock crisis-conversation routes including
   116 123.
   https://www.telefonseelsorge.de/

3. **Hilfetelefon "Gewalt gegen Frauen."** 116 016: free, confidential, around
   the clock, multilingual, telephone and online.
   https://www.hilfetelefon.de/

4. **Hilfetelefon Gewalt an Maennern.** 0800 1239900: anonymous telephone
   support during published service hours, plus online counselling.
   https://www.maennerhilfetelefon.de/

5. **Nummer gegen Kummer.** Child and youth helpline 116 111 and online
   counselling.
   https://www.nummergegenkummer.de/

6. **Behoerdennummer 115.** Nationwide public-administration information line,
   Mon--Fri 08:00--18:00, with telephone, easy-language, and sign-language
   access.
   https://www.115.de/

7. **BBK.** "European emergency number 112," current 2026 information.
   https://www.bbk.bund.de/SharedDocs/Kurzmeldungen/DE/2026/02/om-11-tag-notruf.html

8. **BBK.** "Was tun, wenn es brennt?" Public fire-behaviour guidance.
   https://www.bbk.bund.de/DE/Warnung-Vorsorge/Vorsorge/Brandschutz/_documents/feuer-verhalten_dossier2.html

9. **BBK.** "Vorsorge und Handeln bei Stromausfall." Outage guidance including
   CO and medical-equipment considerations.
   https://www.bbk.bund.de/DE/Warnung-Vorsorge/Vorsorge/Stromausfall/stromausfall_node.html

10. **DVGW.** "Was tun bei Gasgeruch?" Consumer guidance: no flames or
    switches, leave, call from outside.
    https://www.dvgw.de/themen/gas/verbraucherinformationen/was-tun-bei-gasgeruch

11. **gesund.bund.de.** "Erste Hilfe bei Vergiftungen." Stopping exposure,
    fresh air, rinsing, poison-centre consultation, 112 escalation.
    https://gesund.bund.de/erste-hilfe-bei-vergiftungen

12. **BfR.** "Giftinformationszentren in Deutschland." Directory of the seven
    German poison information centres.
    https://www.bfr.bund.de/deutsches-produktregister-depro/giftinformationszentren-in-deutschland/

13. **BBK.** NINA warning-message documentation.
    https://www.bbk.bund.de/DE/Warnung-Vorsorge/Warn-App-NINA/Funktion-Inhalt/Warnmeldungen/warnmeldungen_node.html

15. **gesund.bund.de.** "Sozialpsychiatrischer Dienst -- Hilfe bei psychischen
    Krisen."
    https://gesund.bund.de/sozialpsychiatrischer-dienst

16. **gesund.bund.de.** "Mit psychischen Krisen umgehen." Safety escalation,
    professional support, social connection.
    https://gesund.bund.de/mit-psychischen-krisen-umgehen

17. **WHO.** *Doing What Matters in Times of Stress* (2020). Grounding,
    unhooking, values, and small practical actions.
    https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927

18. **Hofmann SG et al.** Review of cognitive behavioural therapy
    meta-analyses. *Cognitive Therapy and Research* 36 (2012): 427--440.

19. **Shedler J.** Review of psychodynamic psychotherapy evidence. *American
    Psychologist* 65 (2010): 98--109.

20. **Lieberman MD et al.** "Subjective Responses to Emotional Stimuli During
    Labeling, Reappraisal, and Distraction." *Emotion* 11 (2011): 468--480.
    Affect labeling and self-reported affect.
    https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3444304/

21. **Holt-Lunstad J et al.** "Loneliness and social isolation as risk factors
    for mortality: a meta-analytic review." *Perspectives on Psychological
    Science* 10 (2015): 227--237. Adjusted observational ORs: social isolation
    1.29, loneliness 1.26, living alone 1.32.
    https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691614568352

22. **gesund.bund.de.** "Wochenbettdepression." Baby blues versus persistent
    depressive symptoms and professional support.
    https://gesund.bund.de/wochenbettdepression

23. **gesund.bund.de.** "Belastungen pflegender Angehoeriger." Warning signs,
    counselling, respite, and crisis routes.
    https://gesund.bund.de/belastungen-pflegende-angehoerige

    https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/erste-hilfe/veraetzungen/

32. **German Red Cross.** Electrical injury first aid: self-protection, circuit

    https://www.drk.de/hilfe-in-deutschland/erste-hilfe/stromschlag/

33. **Embryotox.** Evidence-based medication information during pregnancy and

    https://www.embryotox.de/

34. **familienplanung.de.** Federal pregnancy and counselling information.

35. **Saver JL.** "Time is brain -- quantified." *Stroke* 37 (2006): 263--266.

    https://doi.org/10.1161/01.STR.0000196957.55928.ab

## Scientific studies

40. **Balban MY et al.** "Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood
    and reduce physiological arousal." *Cell Reports Medicine* 4 (2023):
    100895. 108 adults, four conditions, five minutes daily for 28 days.
    Cyclic sighing showed greater improvement in positive affect than
    mindfulness meditation.
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895

41. **Spitzer RL et al.** "A Brief Measure for Assessing Generalized Anxiety
    Disorder: The GAD-7." *Archives of Internal Medicine* 166 (2006):
    1092--1097. Screening and communication tool, not acute medical triage.
    https://doi.org/10.1001/archinte.166.10.1092

42. **Sarigiannidis I et al.** "Anxiety makes time pass quicker while fear has
    no effect." *Cognition* 197 (2020): 104116. Three experiments found
    anxiety-related underestimation of elapsed duration.
    https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7033556/

43. **Shields GS, Sazma MA, Yonelinas AP.** "The Effects of Acute Stress on
    Core Executive Functions." *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 68
    (2016): 651--668. Meta-analysis of 51 studies and 2,486 participants.
    https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5003767/

44. **Van Dongen HPA et al.** "The cumulative cost of additional wakefulness."
    *Sleep* 26 (2003): 117--126. Chronic sleep restriction produces
    cumulative, dose-dependent performance deficits.
    https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/26.2.117

45. **Cowan N.** "The magical number 4 in short-term memory." *Behavioral and
    Brain Sciences* 24 (2001): 87--114. Working-memory estimates are design
    clues, not fixed personal capacities.
    https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X01003922

46. **Cochrane diagnostic-test-accuracy review (2025).** GAD-7 and GAD-2 for
    detecting anxiety disorders in adults. At cut-off 10: pooled sensitivity
    0.64 (95% CI 0.56--0.72), specificity 0.91 (0.87--0.93) for generalized
    anxiety disorder.
    https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD015455

47. **WHO.** *Infertility Prevalence Estimates, 1990--2021.* Approximately
    17.5% of adults experience infertility during their lifetime.
    https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/infertility

48. **VanderKruik R et al.** "The global prevalence of postpartum psychosis."
    *BMC Psychiatry* 17 (2017): 272. Five incidence studies ranged from 0.89
    to 2.6 per 1,000.
    https://doi.org/10.1186/s12888-017-1427-7

### Editorial note

No philosophical text, screening score, home device, or attractive equation
overrides emergency symptoms or dispatcher instructions. Sources support
specific claims at stated scope. Emergency protocols outrank every chart.
Recheck service numbers and guideline versions before printing.
