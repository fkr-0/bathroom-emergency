# Version History

This guide started as a joke that kept being useful. It has been rewritten
several times, each time because someone actually needed it.

| Version | Date | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| **v2.0** | 2026-04-29 | Initial release. Eight situations, four destination guides, one bathroom. |
| **v2.5** | 2026-04-29 | Added pixel art, expanded chapters, and the personality that made people actually read the thing. |
| **v3.1** | 2026-04-30 | Added scientific formulas and evidence tables. The guide became more rigorous and slightly less fun. |
| **v3.2** | 2026-05-03 | Eight scientific diagrams, 40+ footnotes, full evidence registry. The guide now had more citations than some dissertations and was roughly as enjoyable to read. |
| **v3.3** | 2026-05-03 | Chrome PDF pipeline, template.html, build modernization. The guide's infrastructure was now more robust than most of the situations it described. |
| **v4.0** | 2026-07 | Safety rewrite. Red-flag invariant, evidence registry, Situations G (no safe place) and H (environmental danger). Every emergency route now terminates at a real service. This was necessary and important work. |
| **v4-alt** | 2026-07-22 | Tone restoration branch. Keeps v4 safety architecture, red-flag invariant, evidence standards, and the new situation chapters. Restores v2.5 warmth, humor, and practical personality. Removes decorative formulas, excessive disclaimers, and the 260-line interoception lecture that replaced the quick-start guide. The guide is allowed to be both careful and readable. |

### What v4-alt keeps from v4

- The red-flag invariant (no score or formula may cancel emergency routing)
- Evidence labels on every claim
- Situations G and H
- The complete professional support directory
- All safety-critical routing

### What v4-alt restores from v2.5

- Direct address and warmth
- Humor where it helps (never near a red flag)
- Practical personality over academic caution
- The assumption that a reader in a bathroom deserves both accuracy and
  company

### What v4-alt removes

- Fourteen mathematical formulas where six will do
- The mathematical notation legend
- The full interoception review (relevant, but not here)
- Decorative disclaimers that made every sentence apologize for existing
- The tone of a document that had forgotten who it was for
